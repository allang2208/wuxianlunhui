## Shared prompt contract

- Full-screen game loading background with a broad, calm central horizontal band for the title and progress bar.
- No characters, creatures, text, UI, borders, logos or watermarks.
- Low-saturation palette, large coherent material surfaces, sparse medium-scale weathering, soft atmospheric depth and moderate sharpness.
- Explicitly avoid film grain, digital noise, speckles, stippling, gritty microtexture, crunchy detail, oversharpening, dense particles, chromatic aberration, lens dirt and heavy bloom.
