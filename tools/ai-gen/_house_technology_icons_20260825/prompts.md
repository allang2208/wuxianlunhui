# House technology icon prompt set

Generated with the built-in ImageGen tool on 2026-08-25. The existing
`housing_optimization` technology badge was used as the cold-steel hexagonal
style reference; each accepted LV4-LV7 house image was additionally used as
the corresponding architectural identity reference. The finalizer reconstructs
a true transparent exterior and normalizes every badge to the project-standard
1024x1024 canvas.

- `house_level_2`: compact medieval half-timber residence, gray-stone lower
  floor, aged-plaster upper floor, muted terracotta roof, cyan planning grid
  and one construction chevron.
- `house_level_3`: taller refined half-timber residence, aligned upper tiers,
  restrained balcony, cyan framing diagram and structural chevrons.
- `house_level_4`: elegant compact three-storey small mansion matching the
  accepted LV4 identity, with side and Juliet balconies, dormer, chimney,
  antique-brass details, foundation diagram and final-tier crest.
- `house_level_5`: compact four-storey Victorian steam townhouse with red-brown
  brick, slate mansard roof, bay window, wrought-iron balcony, domestic steam
  riser and gauge.
- `house_level_6`: compact five-storey warm-gray modern apartment with four
  planted balconies, glass lobby, flat parapet roof and two solar assemblies.
- `house_level_7`: compact six-level curved ecological tower with alternating
  elliptical floors, blue-green glass ribbons, three crescent sky gardens,
  observation crown and solar petals.

All three prompts require one connected house, no text, no people, no second
building, no modern architecture, and a cold dark-steel hexagonal technology
badge with restrained cyan technical glow.
