# Corn farm technology icons

> Publication boundary (2026-09-05): this directory is published as a production-source archive only. The normalized runtime icons and the corn-farm technology chain are not part of this archive batch and must be integrated together from the latest `origin/main`.

Generated with the built-in ImageGen tool on 2026-08-28. Both images use the current
World-122 technology-tree cold-steel hexagonal badge family as style-only references.

## `corn_cultivation`

One mature golden corn cob with a partly opened muted-green husk, broad leaves,
orderly cultivated furrows and a restrained old-brass seed-selection ring. Keep the
corn cob dominant and exclude cows, dairy equipment, cheese, wheat, text and modern
machinery.

## `corn_standardization`

Three identical golden corn cobs in a precise symmetric row, backed by an old-brass
calibration ring and accompanied by an orderly grid of identical kernels. The image
must communicate consistent seed grade, harvest timing and processing standards,
with no text, dairy subject, wheat, factory building or modern machinery.

## Shared contract

- 1024 x 1024 technology icon.
- Dark faceted charcoal field inside a worn cold-steel hexagonal frame.
- Restrained cyan status gem and small aged-brass accents.
- Genuine transparent pixels outside the final deterministic hex mask.
- Runtime outputs are normalized to a roughly 1000 px visible badge box.
