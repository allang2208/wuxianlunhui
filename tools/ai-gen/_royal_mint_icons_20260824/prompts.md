# Royal Mint icon prompt set

Shared upgrade style: cold gunmetal square badge, four corner rivets, dark faceted recessed field, aged-brass focal objects, muted slate-blue shadows and restrained amber highlights. The silhouette must remain readable at runtime size; no text. Outside the frame is transparent.

- `mint-precision`: paired engraved coin dies around one crowned brass coin, with a silver measuring caliper.
- `mint-press-speed`: heavy vertical stamping press, brass-and-steel flywheel and short amber speed streaks.
- `mint-energy-efficiency`: sealed crucible furnace, heat-recovery coil and two amber recirculation arrows.
- `mint-staff`: two uniformed hamster mint clerks holding an official ledger and treasury seal.

Shared technology style: 1024x1024 cold-steel hexagonal badge, black faceted recessed field, aged brass and restrained amber luminous connections. Outside the hexagon is transparent.

- `sovereign_minting`: crowned sovereign coin before a fortified royal mint, with crossed scepter and treasury seal.
- `mint_standardization`: central reference coin between matched dies, four identical inspection coins, measuring gauge and gear.

The raw images were generated with the built-in ImageGen workflow. `finalize_icons.py` removes only border-connected canvas pixels, normalizes the visible badge, and installs the runtime assets.
