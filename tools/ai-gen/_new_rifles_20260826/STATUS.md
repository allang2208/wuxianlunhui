## Processing

- Generated in strict side view and muzzle-right; the user then manually cleaned both cutouts and returned the accepted RGBA sources listed above.
- The accepted manual alpha is authoritative. It was not re-cut, repaired, leveled, or flipped.
- Runtime copies were normalized with `tools/ai-gen/add-weapon.py process-image --cutout-tool none --no-orient --no-auto-level`.
- Both runtime images use the existing 2048x2048 weapon canvas contract, centered with the established 88px vertical anchor adjustment.
- STG-44 alpha bounding box: 1873x585, aspect 3.20, 1 connected alpha component.
- QBZ-95 alpha bounding box: 1874x714, aspect 2.62, 1 connected alpha component.
- Inventory icons are rebuilt from the accepted manual RGBA sources by `build-rifle-inventory-icons.py`: 1536² transparent canvas, 35° lower-left-to-upper-right composition, 90% safe occupancy, and a restrained cool rim.
- Defense-tower barrel crops were generated by `tools/prep-tower-barrels.py weapon23 weapon24` using the registered crop contracts.

## Craft sharing

- AKM / STG-44 share 17 base modifications. AKM keeps `heavy_762`, `wood_furniture`, and `side_folding_stock`; STG-44 keeps `stamped_receiver_tuning`, `walnut_fixed_stock`, and `kurz_792_heavy`.
- QBZ-191 / QBZ-95 share 19 base modifications, including `bullpup_balance`. QBZ-191 keeps three modernized-platform modifications; QBZ-95 keeps gas tuning, grip insert, rubber buttpad, and DBP87 balanced ammunition.
- All four rifles use the same four-option sight pool: red dot, 1× prism, 3× tactical, and 4× optical sight.

## Dedicated modification icons and ticket costs

- Ten rifle-specific modification icons are stored in `assets/icons/craft-cold-steel/`, one for every STG-44, QBZ-191, and QBZ-95 exclusive option. Their reproducible ImageGen edit prompts and reference mapping are recorded in `mod-icon-imagegen-prompts.md`.
- Every exclusive option has an explicit `ticketCost` in both crafting mirrors. Regular exclusive parts cost 2 tickets on first installation. `kurz_792_heavy`, `qbz191_high_speed_trigger`, and `qbz95_gas_tuning` are marked `specialModification` and cost 4 tickets.
- Existing replacement behavior remains intact: replacing any installed option in the same slot costs at least 4 tickets. Ticket consumption takes backpack stock first and then warehouse stock.
- Ticket stacks, warehouse contents, and weapon `_craftData` are included by the existing manual save payload. Crafting itself does not trigger a separate immediate autosave.
